How the backdoored models are made. (a) BadNets poisoning: in every training batch a fixed fraction
(5, 8 or 10%, per model) of images get the trigger patch and the target label. (b) The three trigger
patterns. (c) Real predictions (softmax probability) of zoo model m_27c068c4 (white 3x3 patch,
bottom-left, target 'cat', outlined in red) on held-out test images: correct on clean images,
'cat' once the patch is added. (d) Because we plant the backdoor ourselves, every model comes
with a ground-truth answer that the defenders are scored against.
